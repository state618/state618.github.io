申請流程 / HOW IT WORKS

申請之後 / What happens next

送出申請後等待審核，並可隨時使用 Player ID 查詢進度。 / Submit your application, wait for review, and check your progress anytime with your Player ID.

01

送出申請 / Submit your application

填寫移民意願與基本資料，送出你的 618 移民申請。 / Complete your migration interest and basic information, then submit your application.

02

審核與面試 / Review & interview

618 管理層會進行初步審核；若指定聯盟，將由該聯盟管理人員聯繫並安排面試。 / State 618 staff will review your application; if you selected an alliance, its management team will contact you for an interview.

03

查詢進度 / Check your status

使用 Player ID 隨時查詢目前申請進度，並耐心等候後續通知。 / Use your Player ID to check your status anytime and wait for further contact.

申請送出後請耐心等候；如有指定聯盟，後續將由相關聯盟管理人員與你聯繫。 / Please be patient while your application is being reviewed. If you selected a preferred alliance, the appropriate alliance representative will contact you when necessary.
