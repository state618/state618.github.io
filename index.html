HOW IT WORKS · 申請流程

What happens next

Submit your application, wait for review, and check your progress anytime with your Player ID.

01

Submit your application

填寫移民意願與基本資料，送出你的 618 移民申請。

02

Review & interview

618 管理層會進行初步審核；若指定聯盟，將由該聯盟管理人員聯繫並安排面試。

03

Check your status

使用 Player ID 隨時查詢目前申請進度，並耐心等候後續通知。

Please be patient while your application is being reviewed. If you selected a preferred alliance, the appropriate alliance representative will contact you when necessary.
申請送出後請耐心等候；如有指定聯盟，後續將由相關聯盟管理人員與你聯繫。
